# LIVING LEDGER: PECULIAR & RECURRING STUDENT FLAWS
Faculty of Life Sciences / Biotechnology — Departmental Vetting Reference

This living ledger tracks specific, localized, and recurring student writing flaws observed across 400-level project drafts. Cross-reference every submission against these patterns:

1. **Binomial Nomenclature Mutilation**:
   - Writing the specific epithet with a capital letter (e.g., *Escherichia Coli* instead of *Escherichia coli*).
   - Italicizing the abbreviation "sp." or "spp." (e.g., *Aspergillus sp.* instead of *Aspergillus* sp.).
   - Abbreviating genus on first mention without introducing the full name first (e.g., starting directly with *S. aureus* instead of *Staphylococcus aureus*).

2. **Lab Manual / Recipe Imperatives in Chapter 3**:
   - Using direct instructional commands (e.g., *"Weigh 5g of sample and pour into 100ml beaker"* or *"Autoclave at 121°C"*).
   - **Required correction**: Must be written strictly in past passive voice for final projects (*"Five grams (5 g) of sample was weighed and transferred into a 100 mL beaker..."*) or future passive for proposals (*"Five grams (5 g) of sample will be weighed..."*).

3. **Sentences Starting with Arabic Numerals or Abbreviations**:
   - Starting sentences with digits (e.g., *"10 ml of nutrient broth was added..."*).
   - **Required correction**: Numbers starting a sentence must be spelled out in words (*"Ten milliliters (10 mL) of nutrient broth was added..."*).

4. **SI Unit Spacing Violations**:
   - Omitting the required non-breaking space between numerical values and units (e.g., `37°C`, `100rpm`, `5mg/L`, `24hrs`).
   - **Required correction**: `37 °C`, `100 rpm`, `5 mg/L`, `24 h`. Note: percentage `%` has no space (`50%`).

5. **Chapter 4 Literature Contamination**:
   - Citing other authors or literature comparisons in Chapter 4 (Results).
   - **Required correction**: Chapter 4 is exclusively for reporting empirical findings, tables, figures, and statistical significance. All comparisons, justifications, and citations belong strictly in Chapter 5 (Discussion).

6. **Vague Action Verbs in Objectives**:
   - Using unmeasurable verbs like *"To know..."*, *"To understand..."*, *"To study..."*, *"To explore..."*.
   - **Required correction**: Replace with measurable Bloom-level verbs (*"To isolate..."*, *"To quantify..."*, *"To characterize..."*, *"To evaluate..."*).

7. **Missing Experimental Controls & Replicates**:
   - Failure to explicitly state the number of independent replicates (must be n ≥ 3) or negative/positive control groups in Chapter 3.

8. **AI Slop & Rhetorical Fluff**:
   - Overuse of AI markers such as *"delve"*, *"testament"*, *"tapestry"*, *"crucial"*, *"beacon"*, *"plethora"*, *"underscore"*, *"in conclusion"*, or flowery adjectives disconnected from scientific evidence.
