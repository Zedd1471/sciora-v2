# DEPARTMENTAL APPENDICES & PRELIMINARY PAGES GUIDELINES
Faculty of Life Sciences / Biotechnology — Layout & Visual Audit

Use these criteria when auditing preliminary pages, tables, figures, appendices, and reference lists:

1. **Title Page Layout**:
   - Project Title: Centered, Bold, Uppercase, Times New Roman 14-pt, maximum 20–22 words.
   - Student Name: SURNAME, Other Names in Title Case (or ALL CAPS SURNAME).
   - Matriculation/Student ID clearly stated below the name.
   - Institutional Submission Block: Dedicated paragraph stating:
     *"A Research Project Submitted to the Department of Biotechnology / Life Sciences, Faculty of Life Sciences, in Partial Fulfilment of the Requirements for the Award of the Degree of Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. Hons) in Biotechnology..."*
   - Month and Year at the bottom.

2. **Order of Preliminary Pages (Roman Numerals: i, ii, iii...)**:
   - i: Title Page (Roman numeral 'i' is implied but not printed).
   - ii: Certification / Approval Page (signed by Supervisor, Head of Department, External Examiner).
   - iii: Declaration Page (candidate declaration of original work).
   - iv: Dedication.
   - v: Acknowledgements.
   - vi: Abstract (Single paragraph, 250–300 words, single-spaced).
   - vii: Table of Contents.
   - viii: List of Tables.
   - ix: List of Figures.
   - x: List of Plates (if applicable).
   - xi: List of Abbreviations / Acronyms (alphabetical order).

3. **Tables & Figures Typography**:
   - **Table Titles**: Placed **ABOVE** the table, single-spaced, capitalized appropriately (e.g., *Table 4.1: Proximate composition of fermenting substrate*).
   - **Table Body**: 10-point font, clean borders (horizontal rules only; no vertical interior lines per standard academic style).
   - **Figure Legends**: Placed **BELOW** the figure, left-aligned, single-spaced (e.g., *Figure 4.2: Growth kinetics of isolated bacterial strains over 48 hours*).
   - All data values must report standard deviation or standard error (Mean ± SD, n = 3).

4. **References / Bibliography**:
   - Single-spaced within each entry, with double space (or 6 pt paragraph after) between entries.
   - Alphabetical order by first author's surname.
   - Strict author-date system (APA 7th or Harvard Biotechnology standard).
   - Ensure DOI or URL is included where appropriate, without hyperlink underlines.
